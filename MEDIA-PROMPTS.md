# Media prompts

Drop generated files on the paths below. Until a file exists, the page uses a still you already have, or a scene drawn in JavaScript.

Rules for every prompt: photorealistic, no text, no logos, no watermark, no dark grade, no vignette. Keep faces in the middle half of a widescreen frame so a phone can crop the sides.

## Videos (Gemini Veo, 16:9, 6–8 seconds, locked camera)

### `public/video/hero.mp4` (already in the project)

A woman doctor in her early 30s with auburn-red hair pulled back, black rectangular glasses, and a white over-ear headset. Light sky-blue scrubs and a stethoscope. She sits at a white desk with a silver laptop, left of center. White shelves of blue binders, a plant, framed certificates, blinds with cool daylight. She looks at the laptop, lifts her right hand in one calm open-palm gesture, then returns it to the keyboard. Very slow push-in. Face and hand stay in the middle half of the frame.

### `public/video/patient-home.mp4`

A Sri Lankan woman in her 30s sits on a sofa at home, holding a phone at eye level as if on a video visit. Calm, listening, then a small nod. Warm daylight graded cool, white walls, a plant. No readable screen. Slow and quiet.

### `public/video/booking-hands.mp4`

Close-up of hands scrolling a phone on a pale wooden table beside a cup of tea. The screen glows soft blue and shows nothing readable. Slow push-in, shallow depth of field.

### `public/video/doctor-workspace.mp4`

A South Asian male doctor in a white coat at a bright desk with a laptop. He nods while listening, then starts to type. Blue binders on white shelves behind him. Cool daylight, no readable screen text.

### `public/video/family-care.mp4`

A young woman sits beside her elderly father at home. Both look at a tablet during a video visit. The father smiles. Soft window light, white and sky-blue room. Faces stay near the center.
