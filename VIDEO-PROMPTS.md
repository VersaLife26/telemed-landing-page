# Video prompts

The hero plays `/public/video/hero.mp4` when that file exists. Until then, the still at `/public/images/hero-clinician.png` fills the frame and slowly scales.

Generate clips in Gemini (Veo) or similar, 16:9, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Drop the file in as `public/video/hero.mp4`.

## Hero loop (8–10 seconds)

A Sri Lankan woman doctor in soft sky-blue scrubs and a slim white headset sits at a pale desk with a silver laptop. Cool daylight from tall windows, white shelves with blue binders softly out of focus, a small plant. She looks toward the camera, then gestures gently while speaking. Slow push-in, shallow depth of field, premium healthcare commercial, color grade cool blue and white with a hint of mint. No captions.

## Optional second loop

Two people on a calm video consultation, one clinician in a white coat and one patient at home, warm but cool-graded light, slow and quiet, no interface chrome, no readable text on screens.
