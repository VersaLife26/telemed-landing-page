# Video prompts

The hero plays `/public/video/hero.mp4` when that file exists. Until then, the still at `/public/images/hero-clinician.png` fills the frame and slowly scales.

Generate clips in Gemini (Veo) or similar, 16:9, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Drop the file in as `public/video/hero.mp4`.

## Hero loop (8–10 seconds)

One 16:9 file only. Desktop plays the full frame. Phones use the same file and crop the far left and far right, so the desktop composition must stay intact and her face must also survive that crop.

16:9, 8 seconds, locked camera, no text, no logos, no captions, no website UI, no watermark. Photorealistic telehealth hero loop. Do not output a vertical video. Do not center her and lose the wide office.

A woman doctor in her early 30s with auburn-red hair pulled back, black rectangular glasses, and a white over-ear headset with a slim boom microphone. She wears light sky-blue V-neck scrubs and a stethoscope. She sits at a white desk, a silver laptop open in front of her, doctor left of center, laptop toward the right.

Behind her: white cube shelves packed with blue ring binders, a small green plant on the left, framed certificates on the right wall, and a tall window with white blinds throwing cool daylight from the right. On the desk: a black mouse, a short stack of folders, and a clear water bottle at the right edge.

Safe area for mobile: keep her face, glasses, headset, shoulders, and gesturing hand inside the middle half of the frame, with a little space around her head. The shelves on the far left and the window on the far right can be cropped away on a phone. Do not put her face against the left edge.

Action, in order: she looks at the laptop as if speaking on a video call, hands resting near the keyboard. Then she lifts her right hand in one calm open-palm gesture toward the screen, and returns her hand toward the keyboard. Natural blink, small head movement.

Camera stays at eye level, medium-wide, very slow push-in. Shallow depth of field. Cool white and sky-blue grade, soft window light, no green color cast.

Avoid: avocado logo, navigation bar, buttons, readable certificate text, extra people, cartoon look, fast cuts, camera shake, a 9:16 crop as the only frame.
